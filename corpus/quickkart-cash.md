# QuickKart Cash

QuickKart Cash is store credit in your QuickKart account. It is added from refunds you choose to take as store credit and from cashback offers.

You can pay up to 100 percent of an order with QuickKart Cash. It cannot be transferred to a bank account or to another user.

Cashback QuickKart Cash expires 90 days after it is credited. Refund wali QuickKart Cash expire nahi hoti.

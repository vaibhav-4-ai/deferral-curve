# Refund Timeline

Once your returned item is picked up and passes our quality check, we send your refund to your original payment method.

- Standard refunds take 5 to 7 business days after the quality check.
- UPI refunds are usually faster, 2 to 3 business days.
- Credit card and debit card refunds can take up to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

For cash on delivery orders, the refund goes to your bank account. Humein aapki bank details chahiye hongi.

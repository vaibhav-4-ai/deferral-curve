# Damaged Package on Delivery

If the outer package is torn, crushed or wet when it arrives, you can refuse the delivery. The order is then cancelled and refunded.

If you accepted the package and the item inside is broken or damaged in transit, report it within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders with photos of the item and the packaging.

This page covers damage during shipping only. Dabba phata hua ho toh delivery lene se mana kar sakte hain.

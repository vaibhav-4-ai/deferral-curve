# Refunds for Cash on Delivery Orders

For cash on delivery orders, we cannot refund cash. Add your bank account in My Account, then Bank Details, and the refund is sent there by NEFT.

You need the account holder name, account number and IFSC code. The refund reaches your bank within 5 to 7 business days after the bank details are added.

Aap chahein toh refund QuickKart Cash mein bhi le sakte hain, jo turant mil jaata hai.

# EMI Options

EMI is available on credit cards from major banks for orders above 3000 rupees. You can choose 3, 6, 9 or 12 month plans at checkout.

No-cost EMI is available only on selected products marked "No Cost EMI" on the product page.

Debit card EMI is not available. EMI ka interest aapka bank charge karta hai, QuickKart nahi.

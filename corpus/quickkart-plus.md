# QuickKart Plus Membership

QuickKart Plus costs 299 rupees per year. Members get free delivery on every order with no minimum order value, and early access to sales.

Plus membership can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, as long as no Plus benefit has been used.

Membership auto-renew nahi hoti; saal khatam hone par aapko dobara kharidna hoga.

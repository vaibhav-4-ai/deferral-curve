# Order Marked Delivered but Not Received

Sometimes an order shows Delivered before it reaches you. First check with family members, neighbours and your building security.

If you still have not received it, report it from My Orders within 48 hours of the Delivered status by tapping Help, then Not Received. We investigate with the courier and update you within 3 business days.

48 ghante ke andar report karna zaroori hai.

# Payment Failed but Money Deducted

If money was deducted but your order was not placed, the amount is reversed automatically within 5 to 7 business days. You do not need to pay again.

If the amount is not reversed after 7 business days, contact support and share your transaction ID.

Paisa kat gaya ho toh dobara payment na karein, reversal apne aap ho jaata hai.
